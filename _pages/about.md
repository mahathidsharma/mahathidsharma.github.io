I am a biotechnologist working at the interface of virology, immunology and infectious disease. I am currently a **Research Assistant at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru**, where I am standardizing a protocol to rescue Dengue virus from the pFK-DV plasmid.

Before joining IISc, I spent two years as a **Research Associate at Mynvax**, a Bengaluru vaccine start-up, where I worked on preclinical development of Influenza and RSV vaccines: propagating and titrating Influenza virus (HAI, TCID50), running serological assays (HI, ELISA), immunizing hamsters and mice for efficacy studies at IISc's Central Animal Facility, and optimizing qPCR/RT-qPCR SOPs for viral titers.

My earlier research was at CSIR institutes. At **CSIR-IMTech, Chandigarh**, in Dr. Pawan Gupta's lab, I studied host nuclear receptors as targets for host-directed adjunct therapy against tuberculosis, working in BSL-3 with _Mycobacterium tuberculosis_ infection models. This work contributed to our paper in [_Blood Advances_ (2024)](https://doi.org/10.1182/bloodadvances.2023012228) on the nuclear receptor Rev-erbα and erythropoietin regulation. At **CSIR-IGIB, New Delhi**, I was in charge of the sequencing facility (NovaSeq 6000, MiSeq) for the Phenome India gut-microbiome cohort.

I hold an integrated **M.Tech in Biotechnology** from D.Y. Patil Deemed to be University, Navi Mumbai. My thesis, carried out at BRIC-Institute of Life Sciences, Bhubaneswar, examined NCoR1-mediated regulation of mitochondrial function in macrophages infected with _M. tuberculosis_.

Outside the lab, I tutor school students in mathematics and science, volunteer as a scribe for people with disabilities, and am a regular O-negative blood donor (21 donations so far). I speak English, Hindi, Tamil, Kannada and Marathi, and I am learning German (A2) and French.
