**Project Associate (Jan – Jun 2023) and Research Intern (Aug – Dec 2022), CSIR-Institute of Microbial Technology (IMTech), Chandigarh**
Guide: Dr. Pawan Gupta, Chief Scientist

Project: exploring host nuclear receptors as targets for host-directed adjunct therapy to anti-tuberculosis drugs.

- Western blotting to check nuclear receptor expression, and EMSA to study protein-DNA binding.
- Maintained HEK293T, SH-SY5Y and HepG2 cell lines and carried out transfections.
- Confocal microscopy with FITC-labelled peptides to identify cell phenotype alongside co-expressed markers.
- Worked in a Biosafety Level 3 facility: nebulized mice with _Mycobacterium tuberculosis_, then isolated lungs for IHC and CFU counts.
- Mouse breeding and genotyping.

This work contributed to the paper listed under Publications.
