**Research Associate, Mynvax Private Limited, Bengaluru · Dec 2023 – Nov 2025**

- Maintained MDCK and HEp-2 cell lines for virus propagation.
- Propagated Influenza virus stocks and titrated them using the Hemagglutination Inhibition (HAI) and TCID50 assays.
- Ran serological assays: Hemagglutination Inhibition (HI) and ELISA.
- Immunized golden hamsters and BALB/c mice for efficacy studies of RSV and Influenza vaccines at IISc's Central Animal Facility.
- Optimized SOPs for qPCR and RT-qPCR to estimate viral titers of Baculovirus and Rhabdovirus.
