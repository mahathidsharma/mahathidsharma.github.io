**Project Associate, CSIR-Institute of Genomics and Integrative Biology (IGIB), New Delhi · Jul 2023 – Nov 2023**

As part of Phenome India, the CSIR Health cohort knowledgebase, I was in charge of the sequencing facility housing the NovaSeq 6000, MiSeq and Covaris S220.

- Standardized a pan-CSIR protocol for collecting and processing human faecal samples to study gut microbiome diversity and health.
- Isolated sequencing-quality genomic DNA from faecal samples.
- Prepared Whole Genome Sequencing (WGS) libraries for the Illumina NovaSeq 6000.
