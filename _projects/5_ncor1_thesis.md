**Project Trainee, BRIC-Institute of Life Sciences (ILS), Bhubaneswar · Jan – Jul 2022**
Guide: Dr. Sunil Raghav, Scientist F

Thesis: _NCoR1 mediated regulation of mitochondrial function by Mycobacterium tuberculosis in macrophages._

- Generated macrophage-specific NCoR1 knockout mice by crossing dendritic cell and macrophage-specific double knockout mice with NCoR1 floxed mice, and genotyped them (fast DNA extraction, agarose gel electrophoresis).
- Isolated bone-marrow-derived macrophages (BMDMs) from femur and tibia and checked their phenotype by flow cytometry.
- Measured gene expression by RT-qPCR and NCoR1 levels by Western blot.
- Ran the Cell Mito Stress test on a Seahorse XFp analyzer to measure respiratory parameters in BMDMs.
- Cultured and infected BMDMs with _Mycobacterium tuberculosis_ in BSL-2 and BSL-3 facilities.
